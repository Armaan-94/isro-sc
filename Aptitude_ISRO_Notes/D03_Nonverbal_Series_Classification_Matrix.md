# D03. Non-Verbal Series, Classification, Embedded Figures and Figure Matrix

> Every non-verbal question is the same skill: **find what changes and what stays constant**. Run a fixed checklist every time, and don't stop after finding the first change, because harder questions change **two** things at once.

---

## 1. The attribute checklist

Scan in this order:
1. **Count:** sides, dots, lines, elements.
2. **Size:** growing or shrinking.
3. **Shading/colour.**
4. **Rotation/orientation** (by how many degrees per step?).
5. **Position** of a sub-element (moving around corners, clockwise?).
6. **Shape change** (circle → square).

## 2. Series

- Compare **consecutive pairs** (1→2, 2→3, 3→4), not just first and last.
- If no single rule fits, try **alternate steps** (odd and even figures as two interleaved series).
- Rotation tip: after n steps of θ, total rotation = nθ mod 360°. Seven 90° turns = 630° ≡ **270°**.

## 3. Classification (odd one out)

Same checklist; the odd figure breaks a rule the rest share. The difference may be subtle (count parity, symmetry, open vs closed).

## 4. Embedded (hidden) figures

Match the **exact structure**: same number of vertices and segments, same proportions and angles, traceable as **one continuous outline** inside the complex figure. Rough resemblance isn't enough.

## 5. Figure matrix

In a 2 × 2 or 3 × 3 grid, a rule runs **along rows** and often another **down columns**. Find the row rule from complete rows, the column rule from complete columns, and choose the option that satisfies **both**.

Example: dots per cell
```
1  2  3
2  4  6
3  6  ?
```
Each cell = row × column → **9**.

## 6. Pattern completion

Find the symmetry (mirror line or rotation) or the repeating unit, then supply the missing piece that keeps it.

## 7. Transformation vocabulary

| Transformation | What changes |
|---|---|
| Translation | position only |
| Scaling | size only |
| Rotation | orientation (turn about a point) |
| Reflection | handedness (flip across a line) |
| Assembly | pieces combined |
| Grouping | classification by property |
| Folding/cutting | reflections across creases |

## 8. Practice questions (with solutions)

**Q1.** Triangle with 0, 1, 2, 3 dots inside. Next?
(a) square with 4 dots (b) triangle with 4 dots (c) triangle with 3 dots (d) empty triangle
**Answer: (b).**

**Q2.** Odd one: square, rectangle, rhombus, trapezium, circle.
(a) square (b) rectangle (c) circle (d) rhombus
**Answer: (c).**

**Q3.** A figure rotates 90° clockwise each step and gains one shaded corner each step. A student checks only rotation. The mistake?
(a) none (b) stopped after the first attribute; shading also changes (c) two changes are impossible (d) answer needs fewer shaded corners
**Answer: (b).**

**Q4.** A candidate region has 5 sides but its outline isn't one continuous path. Does it contain the hidden pentagon?
(a) yes (b) no (c) can't say (d) yes, vertex count suffices
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** 2 × 2 matrix: 1 circle, 2 circles / 1 square, ?. Rule: add one of the same shape left to right.
(a) 1 square (b) 2 circles (c) 2 squares (d) 1 circle
**Answer: (c).**

**Q6.** In a 3 × 3 matrix, an option fits the row rule. Before choosing it you should:
(a) choose it (b) also check the column rule (c) check only the middle column (d) check the diagonal only
**Answer: (b).**

**Q7.** A circle has 6 sectors alternately shaded (S, U, S, U, S, U). The 4th sector is hidden. It should be:
(a) shaded (b) unshaded (c) half-shaded (d) can't say
**Answer: (b).**

**Q8.** A shape moves to a new grid position without changing size, orientation or shading. Transformation?
(a) rotation (b) scaling (c) translation (d) reflection
**Answer: (c).**

**Q9.** A shape halves in size and rotates 45° clockwise each step. Next figure?
(a) only rotate (b) only shrink (c) halve and rotate 45° (d) impossible series
**Answer: (c).**

**Q10.** An arrow points up, then up-right, then right. Next?
(a) up (b) down-right (c) down (d) left
**Answer: (b).** 45° clockwise each step.

**Q11.** Triangle, square, pentagon, ?
(a) hexagon (b) circle (c) triangle (d) octagon
**Answer: (a).**

**Q12.** Dot matrix rows (1, 2, 3), (2, 4, 6), (3, 6, ?).
(a) 8 (b) 9 (c) 12 (d) 7
**Answer: (b).**

**Q13.** A square has 4 sectors; the shaded sector moves one place clockwise each step, starting at sector 1. Which sector is shaded at step 5?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 3
**Answer: (a).** Steps cycle every 4.

**Q14.** A figure is rotated 90° clockwise at each of 7 steps. Net rotation equals:
(a) 90° clockwise (b) 180° (c) 270° clockwise (d) no change
**Answer: (c).** 630° mod 360°.

**Q15.** A series alternates: odd figures rotate 90°, even figures gain a dot. This is best treated as:
(a) one rule (b) two interleaved series (c) random (d) a matrix
**Answer: (b).**
