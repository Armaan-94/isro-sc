# A05. Ratio and Proportion

> **The core idea.** A ratio says "for every 3 of these there are 5 of those". Turn ratios into **parts** (3k and 5k) and most problems become one-line arithmetic. Proportion properties like componendo-dividendo are shortcuts for equations that look scary but aren't.

---

## 1. Ratio basics

- **Ratio a : b = a/b**: compares two quantities of the **same kind and unit**. It has no unit.
- Convert units first: 5 kg : 200 g = 5000 : 200 = **25 : 1**.
- Multiplying or dividing both terms by the same number doesn't change a ratio.
- In a : b, a is the **antecedent**, b the **consequent**.

### Parts method

If A : B = 3 : 5, write **A = 3k, B = 5k**. Every condition then becomes an equation in k.

---

## 2. Types of ratios

| Type | Of a : b | Example (from 2 : 3) |
|---|---|---|
| Duplicate | a² : b² | 4 : 9 |
| Sub-duplicate | √a : √b | (from 4 : 9) 2 : 3 |
| Triplicate | a³ : b³ | 8 : 27 |
| Sub-triplicate | ∛a : ∛b | (from 8 : 27) 2 : 3 |
| Inverse (reciprocal) | b : a | 3 : 2 |
| Compounded (with c : d) | ac : bd | 2 : 3 with 4 : 5 → 8 : 15 |

---

## 3. Dividing a quantity in a ratio

Total T in ratio m : n : p → parts **T·m/(m + n + p)**, **T·n/(m + n + p)**, **T·p/(m + n + p)**.

720 in 5 : 7 → 300 and 420.

**Fractional ratios:** 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4 → multiply by the LCM 12 → 6 : 4 : 3.

**From mA = nB = pC:** A : B : C = 1/m : 1/n : 1/p. (2A = 3B = 4C → 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4 = **6 : 4 : 3**.)

---

## 4. Combining ratios

A : B = 2 : 3 and B : C = 4 : 5. Make B equal: LCM(3, 4) = 12.
A : B = 8 : 12, B : C = 12 : 15 → **A : B : C = 8 : 12 : 15**.

Shortcut: A : B : C = (a₁·b₂) : (b₁·b₂) : (b₁·c₂) = 2·4 : 3·4 : 3·5 = 8 : 12 : 15.

A : C = (A/B) × (B/C) = (2/3)(4/5) = 8/15.

---

## 5. Proportion

a : b :: c : d means a/b = c/d. **Product of extremes = product of means: ad = bc.**

- **Fourth proportional** to a, b, c: x = bc/a. (3, 5, 9 → 15)
- **Third proportional** to a, b: a : b :: b : x → x = b²/a. (4, 8 → 16)
- **Mean proportional** between a and b: √(ab). (9, 16 → 12)

### Properties (given a/b = c/d)

| Name | Result |
|---|---|
| Invertendo | b/a = d/c |
| Alternendo | a/c = b/d |
| Componendo | (a + b)/b = (c + d)/d |
| Dividendo | (a − b)/b = (c − d)/d |
| **Componendo-dividendo** | **(a + b)/(a − b) = (c + d)/(c − d)** |
| Addendo | a/b = c/d = e/f = (a + c + e)/(b + d + f) |

**Componendo-dividendo in reverse:** if (x + y)/(x − y) = p/q then **x/y = (p + q)/(p − q)**.

Example: (5x + 3y)/(5x − 3y) = 4/1 → 5x/3y = 5/3 → x/y = 1/1.
Example: (a + b)/(a − b) = 7/3 → a/b = 10/4 = 5/2.

---

## 6. Variation

| Type | Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Direct | y = kx | y/x constant: double x, double y |
| Inverse | y = k/x | xy constant: double x, halve y |
| Joint | z = kxy | |

Time ∝ distance (speed fixed): direct. Time ∝ 1/speed (distance fixed): inverse. Men ∝ 1/days (work fixed): inverse.

---

## 7. Comparing ratios

a/b vs c/d: compare **ad and bc** (cross-multiply).
3/7 vs 5/11: 33 vs 35 → **5/11** is larger.

---

## 8. Exam traps

1. Units must match before writing a ratio.
2. Combining ratios: equalise the common term via LCM.
3. mA = nB = pC → reciprocals.
4. Speed ratio a : b → time ratio b : a (same distance).
5. Componendo-dividendo is the fastest route for (x + y)/(x − y) equations.

## 9. Practice questions (with solutions)

**Q1.** Divide 720 in the ratio 5 : 7.
(a) 300, 420 (b) 320, 400 (c) 350, 370 (d) 310, 410
**Answer: (a).**

**Q2.** Duplicate ratio of 3 : 5?
(a) 6:10 (b) 9:25 (c) 3:25 (d) 9:5
**Answer: (b).**

**Q3.** a : b = 2 : 3, b : c = 4 : 5. a : b : c = ?
(a) 8:12:15 (b) 2:3:5 (c) 8:15:12 (d) 6:9:15
**Answer: (a).**

**Q4.** Two numbers in ratio 3 : 4 have sum 84. The numbers?
(a) 36, 48 (b) 30, 54 (c) 32, 52 (d) 40, 44
**Answer: (a).**

**Q5.** (5x + 3y) : (5x − 3y) = 4 : 1. x : y = ?
(a) 1:1 (b) 3:5 (c) 5:3 (d) 2:3
**Answer: (a).**

**Q6.** 2A = 3B = 4C. A : B : C = ?
(a) 6:4:3 (b) 2:3:4 (c) 3:4:6 (d) 4:3:2
**Answer: (a).**

**Q7.** The sum of two numbers is 8 times their difference. Larger : smaller?
(a) 9:7 (b) 7:5 (c) 5:3 (d) 3:1
**Answer: (a).** x + y = 8(x − y) → 9y = 7x.

**Q8.** Third proportional to 4 and 8?
(a) 12 (b) 16 (c) 32 (d) 2
**Answer: (b).**

**Q9.** Mean proportional between 9 and 16?
(a) 12 (b) 12.5 (c) 25 (d) 144
**Answer: (a).**

**Q10.** Fourth proportional to 3, 5, 9?
(a) 12 (b) 15 (c) 27 (d) 45
**Answer: (b).**

**Q11.** Compounded ratio of 2 : 3, 4 : 5 and 5 : 8?
(a) 1:3 (b) 11:16 (c) 1:2 (d) 2:5
**Answer: (a).** (2 × 4 × 5) : (3 × 5 × 8) = 40 : 120 = 1 : 3.

**Q12.** Sub-duplicate ratio of 49 : 64?
(a) 7:8 (b) 49:8 (c) 14:16 (d) 2401:4096
**Answer: (a).**

**Q13.** A : B = 3 : 4, B : C = 6 : 7. A : C = ?
(a) 9:14 (b) 3:7 (c) 1:2 (d) 18:21
**Answer: (a).** A : B : C = 9 : 12 : 14.

**Q14.** ₹1300 is divided among A, B, C in the ratio 1/2 : 1/3 : 1/4. C's share?
(a) ₹300 (b) ₹400 (c) ₹600 (d) ₹325
**Answer: (a).** 6 : 4 : 3 → C = 1300 × 3/13.

**Q15.** What number must be added to each of 6, 14, 18, 38 so that they become proportional?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
**Answer: (b).** (6 + x)(38 + x) = (14 + x)(18 + x) → 12x = 24.

**Q16.** Two numbers are in ratio 3 : 5. Subtracting 9 from each gives ratio 12 : 23. The smaller number?
(a) 27 (b) 33 (c) 49 (d) 55
**Answer: (b).** (3k − 9)/(5k − 9) = 12/23 → k = 11.

**Q17.** Salaries of A and B are in ratio 2 : 3. Each gets a raise of ₹4000 and the ratio becomes 40 : 57. B's new salary?
(a) ₹34,000 (b) ₹38,000 (c) ₹40,000 (d) ₹36,000
**Answer: (b).** 57(2x + 4000) = 40(3x + 4000) → 6x = 68000 → 3x = 34000 → new 38000.

**Q18.** A bag has ₹1, 50 p and 25 p coins in number ratio 5 : 6 : 8, totalling ₹210. Number of 25 p coins?
(a) 105 (b) 126 (c) 168 (d) 84
**Answer: (c).** Value ratio 5 : 3 : 2 → 25 p coins are worth 42 → 168 coins.

**Q19.** x : y = 3 : 4. (4x + 5y) : (5x − 2y) = ?
(a) 32:7 (b) 7:32 (c) 16:7 (d) 32:13
**Answer: (a).** (12 + 20) : (15 − 8).

**Q20.** Speeds of two trains are in ratio 3 : 4. Ratio of times to cover the same distance?
(a) 3:4 (b) 4:3 (c) 9:16 (d) 16:9
**Answer: (b).**

**Q21.** a/3 = b/4 = c/7. (a + b + c)/c = ?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 14/3
**Answer: (b).**

**Q22.** x varies inversely as y; x = 12 when y = 5. y when x = 15?
(a) 4 (b) 6 (c) 3 (d) 6.25
**Answer: (a).** xy = 60.

**Q23.** (a + b)/(a − b) = 7/3. a : b = ?
(a) 5:2 (b) 7:3 (c) 2:5 (d) 10:3
**Answer: (a).**

**Q24.** Which is greater: 3/7 or 5/11?
(a) 3/7 (b) 5/11 (c) equal (d) can't say
**Answer: (b).**

**Q25.** 40 L of milk-water mixture has milk : water = 5 : 3. Water to be added to make it 1 : 1?
(a) 5 L (b) 8 L (c) 10 L (d) 15 L
**Answer: (c).** Milk 25, water 15.

**Q26.** Ages of A and B are in ratio 4 : 5. After 5 years the ratio is 5 : 6. A's present age?
(a) 16 (b) 20 (c) 25 (d) 24
**Answer: (b).** (4x + 5)/(5x + 5) = 5/6 → x = 5.

**Q27.** If 15 men can do a job in 8 days, how many days will 20 men take (same work)?
(a) 6 (b) 10 (c) 5 (d) 12
**Answer: (a).** Inverse variation: 15 × 8 = 20 × d.

**Q28.** If A : B = 5 : 7 and B : C = 3 : 4, then A : B : C = ?
(a) 15:21:28 (b) 5:7:4 (c) 15:21:24 (d) 5:21:28
**Answer: (a).**
