# C08. Blood Relations and Directions

> **Blood relations:** draw a tiny family tree with generations as levels; don't try to hold the chain in your head. **Directions:** turn every walk into (x, y) coordinates; don't try to visualise the path.

---

## 1. Blood relations

### Vocabulary

| Phrase | Means |
|---|---|
| Father's/mother's son | Brother (or the speaker himself) |
| Father's/mother's daughter | Sister (or the speaker herself) |
| Mother's/father's father | Grandfather |
| Father's sister | Paternal aunt |
| Mother's brother | Maternal uncle |
| Brother's/sister's son | Nephew |
| Brother's/sister's daughter | Niece |
| Son's wife | Daughter-in-law |
| Spouse's brother / sister's husband | Brother-in-law |
| Spouse's father | Father-in-law |
| Uncle's/aunt's child | Cousin |

### Method

1. **Start from the end of the chain** closest to the known person and decode outward.
2. Draw generations: grandparents on top, parents, then the speaker's generation, then children. Use + for male, − for female, = for married couples, a horizontal line for siblings.
3. Keep gender **unknown** until a word fixes it.
4. **Self-reference check:** "my mother's only daughter" is the speaker (if female). "My father's only son" is the speaker (if male).

**Examples:**
- "His mother is my mother's only daughter" (woman speaking) → his mother is the speaker → he is her **son**.
- "Her wife's..." no: "His wife is the only daughter of my mother" (woman speaking) → his wife is the speaker → he is her **husband**.
- "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather" → the only son of my grandfather is my father → he is my **brother**.
- "Your mother's husband's sister is my aunt" → your father's sister is my aunt → we are **cousins** (or siblings; the standard answer is cousin).
- P is Q's mother; Q is R's brother; R is S's son → S is R's father → S is P's **husband**.

### Coded relations

The symbol meanings are always given; translate one link at a time.
A $ B: A is B's father. A # B: A is B's wife. P $ Q # R → P is Q's father; Q is R's wife → P is R's **father-in-law**.

## 2. Directions

```
           N
     NW         NE
  W       +       E
     SW         SE
           S
```

- **Right turn** (clockwise): N → E → S → W → N.
- **Left turn** (anticlockwise): N → W → S → E → N.
- A 180° turn reverses direction; turning 135° clockwise from N gives SE.

### Coordinate method

East = +x, West = −x, North = +y, South = −y. Add the legs, then:
- Straight-line distance = √(x² + y²).
- Direction from the signs: (+, +) NE, (+, −) SE, (−, +) NW, (−, −) SW; (+, 0) East, etc.

**Example:** 5 N, turn right 3, turn right 5, turn left 4.
(0, 5) → facing E → (3, 5) → facing S → (3, 0) → left of south is E → (7, 0). **7 km East.**

**Example:** 3 km E, 4 km N → (3, 4) → **5 km NE**.

### Shadows

The shadow falls **away from the sun**.
- Morning (sun in the east): shadow points **west**.
- Evening (sun in the west): shadow points **east**.
- Noon: shortest shadow; sunrise/sunset: longest.

Morning, shadow to his left → west is on his left → he faces **north**.

---

## 3. Exam traps

1. Self-reference ("my mother's only daughter").
2. Gender isn't given by a name.
3. A left turn while facing south points east, not west.
4. Shadow direction: away from the sun.

## 4. Practice questions (with solutions)

**Q1.** Pointing to a man, a woman says, "His mother is my mother's only daughter." The man is her:
(a) brother (b) son (c) father (d) uncle
**Answer: (b).**

**Q2.** A is B's father; B is C's brother. A is C's:
(a) father (b) grandfather (c) uncle (d) brother
**Answer: (a).**

**Q3.** A man points to a photo: "She is the daughter of my grandfather's only son." She is his:
(a) sister (b) wife (c) daughter (d) mother
**Answer: (a).**

**Q4.** A man says to a woman, "Your mother's husband's sister is my aunt." She is his:
(a) sister (b) cousin (c) niece (d) mother
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** P is Q's mother. Q is R's brother. R is S's son. S is P's:
(a) husband (b) brother (c) daughter (d) can't say
**Answer: (a).**

**Q6.** A + B: A is B's mother; A × B: A is B's brother; A − B: A is B's father. P + Q × R − S. S is P's:
(a) grandchild (b) son (c) grandfather (d) nephew
**Answer: (a).**

**Q7.** 5 km N, right 3 km, right 5 km, left 4 km. Final position from start?
(a) 7 km E (b) 7 km N (c) 9 km E (d) 3 km N
**Answer: (a).**

**Q8.** 8 km E, 6 km S, 8 km W. Position from start?
(a) 6 km N (b) 6 km S (c) 10 km SW (d) 14 km S
**Answer: (b).**

**Q9.** In the morning a man's shadow falls to his left. He faces:
(a) north (b) south (c) east (d) west
**Answer: (a).**

**Q10.** A tower's shadow is longer than its height. Most likely time?
(a) noon (b) near sunrise or sunset (c) unrelated (d) can't say
**Answer: (b).**

**Q11.** A woman says of a man, "His wife is the only daughter of my mother." He is her:
(a) brother (b) husband (c) son-in-law (d) father
**Answer: (b).**

**Q12.** A is B's sister. C is B's mother. D is C's father. E is D's mother. A is D's:
(a) granddaughter (b) daughter (c) grandmother (d) niece
**Answer: (a).**

**Q13.** Meena points to a boy: "He is the son of the only son of my grandfather." He is her:
(a) cousin (b) brother (c) nephew (d) uncle
**Answer: (b).**

**Q14.** M is N's brother. N is O's sister. O is P's father. M is P's:
(a) uncle (b) father (c) brother (d) grandfather
**Answer: (a).**

**Q15.** Walk 10 m N, turn left 5 m, turn left 10 m. Position?
(a) 5 m E (b) 5 m W (c) 10 m S (d) at start
**Answer: (b).**

**Q16.** 3 km E then 4 km N. Distance and direction from start?
(a) 7 km NE (b) 5 km NE (c) 5 km NW (d) 1 km N
**Answer: (b).**

**Q17.** Facing east, turn 90° clockwise, then 180°. Now facing:
(a) north (b) south (c) east (d) west
**Answer: (a).** East → south → north.

**Q18.** Facing north, turn 135° clockwise. Now facing:
(a) south-east (b) south-west (c) north-east (d) east
**Answer: (a).**

**Q19.** In the evening a man faces his own shadow. He faces:
(a) east (b) west (c) north (d) south
**Answer: (a).**

**Q20.** A $ B: A is B's father. A # B: A is B's wife. P $ Q # R. P is R's:
(a) father (b) father-in-law (c) brother-in-law (d) grandfather
**Answer: (b).**

**Q21.** Facing north, turn right, right, then left. Now facing:
(a) north (b) east (c) south (d) west
**Answer: (b).** N → E → S → E.

**Q22.** Ravi walks 6 km S, turns right 8 km. Straight-line distance from start?
(a) 14 km (b) 10 km (c) 2 km (d) 12 km
**Answer: (b).** (−8, −6).
