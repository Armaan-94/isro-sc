# General Aptitude, Engineering Maths and Reasoning: Deep-Dive Notes

Beginner-friendly notes for the aptitude part of the ISRO Scientist/Engineer 'SC' (Computer Science) exam. Every chapter builds the idea from scratch, gives you the shortcuts and the traps, and ends with a large practice set where every answer has been checked by hand.

**How to use:** read the concept sections once, then attempt the practice questions without looking at the answers. Mark what you get wrong and re-read only that section. Expect roughly 1,000 questions in total.

## Corrections to the original PDF

A few answers and rules in the source PDF were wrong. These notes use the corrected versions:

- **Water image of a clock** is 18:30 − time, not 6:00 − time (a top-bottom flip turns minute m into 30 − m). For example, 10:10 → 8:20.
- **Circular seating:** facing the centre, your right is **anticlockwise**; facing outward, your right is **clockwise**.
- **Inequality chains:** A > B ≥ C does give A > C. One strict link in a chain running one way makes the conclusion strict.
- **Paper folding:** punching the centre of a folded quarter-sheet gives 4 holes, not 1. You get 1 hole only when you punch exactly where the creases meet.
