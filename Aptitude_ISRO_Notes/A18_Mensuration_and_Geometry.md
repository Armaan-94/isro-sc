# A18. Mensuration and Geometry

> Mensuration is **formula recall plus careful reading**. Most errors come from using total surface area when the question wants curved surface area, or forgetting to convert units. Learn the tables below, memorise the Pythagorean triples, and know how areas and volumes **scale**.

---

## 1. 2D shapes

| Shape | Area | Perimeter |
|---|---|---|
| Square (side a) | a² (also d²/2) | 4a |
| Rectangle (l, b) | lb | 2(l + b) |
| Triangle | ½ × base × height | a + b + c |
| Triangle (Heron), s = (a + b + c)/2 | √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)] | a + b + c |
| Equilateral (side a) | (√3/4)a² | 3a |
| Circle (r) | πr² | 2πr |
| Sector (angle θ) | (θ/360)πr² | arc = (θ/360)2πr |
| Parallelogram | base × height | 2(sum of adjacent sides) |
| Rhombus (diagonals d₁, d₂) | ½ d₁d₂ | 4 × side |
| Trapezium (parallel a, b; height h) | ½(a + b)h | sum of sides |

Diagonal of a rectangle = √(l² + b²); of a square = a√2.

**Examples:**
- Equilateral triangle, side 8: (√3/4) × 64 = **16√3**.
- Triangle 13, 14, 15: s = 21 → √(21 × 8 × 7 × 6) = √7056 = **84**.
- Square with diagonal 10: 100/2 = **50**.
- Circumference 44 → r = 7 → area **154** (π = 22/7).
- Sector r = 7, 90°: ¼ × 154 = **38.5**.

## 2. 3D solids

| Solid | Volume | Curved/lateral SA | Total SA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cube (a) | a³ | 4a² | 6a² |
| Cuboid (l, b, h) | lbh | 2h(l + b) | 2(lb + bh + hl) |
| Cylinder (r, h) | πr²h | 2πrh | 2πr(r + h) |
| Cone (r, h, slant l = √(r² + h²)) | ⅓πr²h | πrl | πr(l + r) |
| Sphere (r) | (4/3)πr³ | 4πr² | 4πr² |
| Hemisphere (r) | (2/3)πr³ | 2πr² | 3πr² |

Space diagonal of a cuboid = √(l² + b² + h²); of a cube = a√3.

> **Trap:** "curved" or "lateral" surface excludes the flat faces. Painting the outside of a hemispherical bowl = **2πr²**, not 3πr².

**Recasting (melting one solid into another): volume is conserved.**
Sphere of radius 3 (volume 36π) recast into a cylinder of radius 3 → 9πh = 36π → **h = 4**.

**Small cubes from a big cube:** side 6 into side-2 cubes → (6/2)³ = **27**.

**Wheels:** distance per revolution = circumference. Wheel diameter 70 cm → 220 cm. Revolutions for 1.1 km = 110,000/220 = **500**.

## 3. How things scale

If every length is multiplied by k:
- **Areas × k²**, **volumes × k³**.
- Cube side doubled → volume × 8.
- Radius up 10% → area × 1.21 → **+21%** (successive change 10 + 10 + 1).
- Similar triangles with sides 2 : 3 → areas **4 : 9**.

Rectangle: length +20%, breadth −20% → area × 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 → **−4%**.

## 4. Geometry facts

### Triangles
- Angle sum 180°; exterior angle = sum of the two opposite interior angles.
- **Pythagorean triples:** 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25 and multiples (6-8-10, 9-12-15, 10-24-26).
- Triangle inequality: any side < sum of the other two.

### Polygons
- Interior angle sum = **(n − 2) × 180°**. Hexagon: 720°, each 120° if regular.
- Exterior angles always sum to **360°**. Each exterior angle 30° → **12 sides**.
- Diagonals = n(n − 3)/2.

### Circles
- **Angle in a semicircle = 90°.**
- Angle at the centre = **2 ×** angle at the circumference on the same arc (inscribed 35° → central **70°**).
- Angles in the same segment are equal.
- Tangent ⟂ radius at the point of contact.
- Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to 180°.

---

## 5. Exam traps

1. CSA vs TSA.
2. Units: 1 m³ = 1000 L; 1 m = 100 cm.
3. Use the slant height l for a cone's surface, the vertical height h for its volume.
4. Scaling: area squares, volume cubes.
5. π = 22/7 when radii are multiples of 7.
