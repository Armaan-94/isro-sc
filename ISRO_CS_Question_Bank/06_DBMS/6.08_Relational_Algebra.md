# 6.8 Relational Algebra

**Notes:** [08 Relational Algebra](../../DBMS_ISRO_Notes/08_Relational_Algebra.md)

> 24 questions. RA uses set semantics (no duplicates).

---

**Q1.** Removes duplicates by default:
(a) select (b) project (c) Cartesian product (d) theta join
**Answer: (b).**

**Q2.** R: 5 tuples, degree 3. S: 4 tuples, degree 2. R × S degree and cardinality?
(a) 5, 9 (b) 6, 9 (c) 5, 20 (d) 6, 20
**Answer: (c).**

**Q3.** R ∪ S requires:
(a) same number of tuples (b) union compatibility (same degree, matching domains) (c) a shared attribute name (d) identical keys
**Answer: (b).**

**Q4.** Keeps all tuples of both relations:
(a) natural (b) left outer (c) right outer (d) full outer join
**Answer: (d).**

**Q5.** "Suppliers who supply ALL parts":
(a) natural join (b) division (c) difference (d) product
**Answer: (b).**

**Q6.** Joining on dept_id while keeping both dept_id columns is:
(a) natural join (b) equi-join (c) left outer join (d) invalid
**Answer: (b).**

**Q7.** Intersection is:
(a) fundamental (b) derived: R − (R − S) (c) needs product (d) numeric only
**Answer: (b).**

**Q8.** R(A, B, C) ÷ S(B, C) gives attributes:
(a) A, B, C (b) B, C (c) A (d) none
**Answer: (c).**

**Q9.** Not an outer join:
(a) left (b) right (c) full (d) Cartesian outer join
**Answer: (d).**

**Q10.** R = {(1, a), (2, a), (3, b)} over (A, B). Number of tuples in Π_B(R)?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 0
**Answer: (b).** {a, b}.

**Q11.** R(A, B) with 3 tuples, S(B, C) with 4 tuples. Possible cardinality of R ⋈ S?
(a) exactly 12 (b) 0 to 12 (c) 3 to 4 (d) 7
**Answer: (b).**

**Q12.** Natural join of relations with no common attribute equals:
(a) empty (b) Cartesian product (c) union (d) intersection
**Answer: (b).**

**Q13.** |R| = 5, |S| = 4, union-compatible. Range of |R ∪ S|?
(a) 4 to 9 (b) 5 to 9 (c) 0 to 9 (d) exactly 9
**Answer: (b).**

**Q14.** Same. Range of |R ∩ S|?
(a) 0 to 4 (b) 0 to 5 (c) 4 to 5 (d) 1 to 4
**Answer: (a).**

**Q15.** Same. Range of |R − S|?
(a) 0 to 5 (b) 1 to 5 (c) 0 to 4 (d) exactly 1
**Answer: (b).** At least 5 − 4 = 1.

**Q16.** Enrolled(S, C) = {(s1, c1), (s1, c2), (s2, c1), (s3, c1), (s3, c2)}; Courses = {c1, c2}. Enrolled ÷ Courses?
(a) {s1} (b) {s1, s3} (c) {s1, s2, s3} (d) {s2}
**Answer: (b).**

**Q17.** A self-join needs which operator?
(a) rename (ρ) (b) division (c) union (d) difference
**Answer: (a).**

**Q18.** |R ⟕ S| (left outer join) is at least:
(a) |S| (b) |R| (c) 0 (d) |R| × |S|
**Answer: (b).**

**Q19.** A complete set of RA operators:
(a) σ, Π, ∪, −, ×, ρ (b) σ, Π only (c) ⋈, ÷ only (d) ∩, ∪
**Answer: (a).** Join, intersection and division are derivable.

**Q20.** σ selects:
(a) rows satisfying a condition (b) columns (c) duplicates (d) keys
**Answer: (a).**

**Q21.** σ_{p1}(σ_{p2}(R)) equals:
(a) σ_{p1 ∧ p2}(R) (b) σ_{p1 ∨ p2}(R) (c) Π(R) (d) R
**Answer: (a).** Selections commute and combine.

**Q22.** RA is:
(a) procedural (b) declarative like TRC (c) a programming language (d) a storage format
**Answer: (a).**

**Q23.** Degree of R ⋈ S where R(A, B, C), S(C, D):
(a) 5 (b) 4 (c) 3 (d) 6
**Answer: (b).** The common column C appears once.

**Q24.** Π_A(σ_{B = 'x'}(R)) in SQL is:
(a) SELECT DISTINCT A FROM R WHERE B = 'x' (b) SELECT B FROM R WHERE A = 'x' (c) SELECT * FROM R (d) DELETE FROM R
**Answer: (a).** DISTINCT matches RA's set semantics.
