# 6.11 Tuple and Domain Relational Calculus

**Notes:** [11 Tuple and Domain Relational Calculus](../../DBMS_ISRO_Notes/11_Tuple_and_Domain_Relational_Calculus.md)

> 18 questions.

---

**Q1.** RA vs relational calculus:
(a) RA declarative (b) RA procedural, calculus declarative (c) identical (d) calculus can't join
**Answer: (b).**

**Q2.** An unquantified tuple variable is:
(a) bound (b) free (c) universal (d) undefined
**Answer: (b).**

**Q3.** Customers with a loan but no account:
(a) ∃ borrower OR ∃ depositor (b) ∃ borrower AND ∃ depositor (c) ∃ borrower AND NOT ∃ depositor (d) NOT ∃ borrower
**Answer: (c).**

**Q4.** DRC query with a degree-3 result needs:
(a) 1 variable (b) 3 domain variables (c) one per relation (d) none
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** {t | NOT (t ∈ r)} is unsafe because:
(a) bad syntax (b) it may describe an infinite set (c) NOT is banned (d) only for empty r
**Answer: (b).**

**Q6.** ∀ corresponds to which RA operator?
(a) σ (b) Π (c) ÷ (d) ×
**Answer: (c).**

**Q7.** DRC underlies:
(a) SQL (b) QBE (c) RA (d) PL/SQL
**Answer: (b).**

**Q8.** {t | t ∈ loan ∧ t[amount] > 1200} returns:
(a) loans over 1200 (b) amounts only (c) borrowers (d) all loans
**Answer: (a).** Whole tuples.

**Q9.** In TRC, variables range over:
(a) whole tuples (b) single attribute values (c) relations (d) operators
**Answer: (a).** In DRC, over domain values.

**Q10.** ∀t ∈ r (P(t)) is equivalent to:
(a) ¬∃t ∈ r (¬P(t)) (b) ∃t ∈ r (P(t)) (c) ¬∀t (P) (d) ∃t (¬P)
**Answer: (a).**

**Q11.** P₁ ⇒ P₂ is equivalent to:
(a) ¬P₁ ∨ P₂ (b) P₁ ∧ ¬P₂ (c) ¬P₁ ∧ P₂ (d) P₁ ∨ P₂
**Answer: (a).**

**Q12.** Customers with a loan OR an account:
(a) ∃ borrower ∨ ∃ depositor (b) ∃ borrower ∧ ∃ depositor (c) ∃ borrower ∧ ¬∃ depositor (d) ¬∃ both
**Answer: (a).**

**Q13.** Safe relational calculus and relational algebra are:
(a) equally expressive (relationally complete) (b) RA stronger (c) calculus stronger (d) unrelated
**Answer: (a).** Codd's theorem.

**Q14.** {t.RollNo | Student(t) ∧ t.branch = 'CSE'} returns:
(a) full CSE student tuples (b) only roll numbers of CSE students (c) all roll numbers (d) branches
**Answer: (b).**

**Q15.** In DRC, a condition like s > 80000 compares:
(a) a domain variable with a constant (b) two relations (c) two tuples (d) a tuple with a relation
**Answer: (a).**

**Q16.** SQL's theoretical foundations are:
(a) RA and TRC (b) only DRC (c) only RA (d) set theory only
**Answer: (a).**

**Q17.** A quantified variable is called:
(a) free (b) bound (c) safe (d) prime
**Answer: (b).**

**Q18.** Main sources of unsafe calculus expressions:
(a) negation and universal quantification not bounded by a relation (b) projection (c) constants (d) AND
**Answer: (a).**
