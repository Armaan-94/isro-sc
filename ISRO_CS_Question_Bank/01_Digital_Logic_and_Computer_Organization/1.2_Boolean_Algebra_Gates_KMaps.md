# 1.2 Boolean Algebra, Logic Gates and K-Map Minimization

> 30 questions. Attempt first, then check the explanation.

---

**Q1.** A universal gate:
(a) AND (b) OR (c) NAND (d) XOR
**Answer: (c).** NAND alone can build NOT, AND and OR, so any function. NOR is also universal.

**Q2.** (A + B)' equals:
(a) A' + B' (b) A'B' (c) AB (d) A' + B
**Answer: (b).** De Morgan: complement of an OR is the AND of complements.

**Q3.** The dual of A + 1 = 1 is:
(a) A' + 1 = 1 (b) A · 0 = 0 (c) A · 1 = 1 (d) A + 0 = A
**Answer: (b).** Swap + ↔ · and 0 ↔ 1; variables stay as they are.

**Q4.** K-map cells use Gray-code order because:
(a) it shrinks the map (b) adjacent cells must differ in exactly one variable (c) it matches decimal order (d) it's needed only for don't-cares
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** An essential prime implicant:
(a) covers the most minterms (b) covers at least one minterm no other PI covers (c) has the fewest literals (d) is always chosen first regardless
**Answer: (b).**

**Q6.** A don't-care in a K-map should be:
(a) always 1 (b) always 0 (c) used as 1 only if it enlarges a group (d) ignored
**Answer: (c).**

**Q7.** A + AB = ?
(a) A (b) B (c) AB (d) A + B
**Answer: (a).** Absorption: A(1 + B) = A.

**Q8.** Minimum NAND gates to make a NOT gate?
(a) impossible (b) 1, with inputs tied (c) 2 (d) 3
**Answer: (b).** (A·A)' = A'.

**Q9.** In AB + A'C + BC, the redundant term is:
(a) AB (b) A'C (c) BC (d) none
**Answer: (c).** Consensus theorem: AB + A'C + BC = AB + A'C.

**Q10.** A + A'B = ?
(a) A (b) A + B (c) AB (d) A'B
**Answer: (b).** (A + A')(A + B) = A + B.

**Q11.** (A + B)(A + C) = ?
(a) A + BC (b) AB + C (c) A + B + C (d) ABC
**Answer: (a).** Distributive law of OR over AND.

**Q12.** A ⊕ 1 = ?
(a) A (b) A' (c) 1 (d) 0
**Answer: (b).** XOR with 1 inverts; XOR with 0 passes through.

**Q13.** A ⊕ A = ?
(a) A (b) 1 (c) 0 (d) A'
**Answer: (c).**

**Q14.** Minimum 2-input NOR gates to build an AND gate?
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
**Answer: (b).** NOR each input with itself to get A' and B', then NOR them: (A' + B')' = AB.

**Q15.** Minimum 2-input NAND gates for XOR?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 5 (d) 6
**Answer: (b).** XOR needs 4 NANDs; with NOR gates it needs 5.

**Q16.** Number of distinct Boolean functions of 2 variables?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 16 (d) 32
**Answer: (c).** 2^(2ⁿ) = 2⁴.

**Q17.** F(A, B, C) = Σm(0, 1, 2, 3) minimises to:
(a) A (b) A' (c) B' (d) C'
**Answer: (b).** All minterms with A = 0.

**Q18.** F(A, B, C) = Σm(1, 3, 5, 7) minimises to:
(a) A (b) B (c) C (d) A'C
**Answer: (c).** All odd minterms: the LSB C = 1.

**Q19.** F(A, B, C, D) = Σm(0, 2, 8, 10) minimises to:
(a) A'C' (b) B'D' (c) BD (d) A'B'
**Answer: (b).** The four corners of the K-map: B = 0 and D = 0.

**Q20.** F(A, B, C) = Σm(0, 1, 2, 5, 6, 7). How many prime implicants does it have?
(a) 3 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 2
**Answer: (c).** A'B', A'C', BC', AB, AC, B'C: a cyclic map. Every minimum SOP uses 3 of them, and none is essential.

**Q21.** Complement of AB + C'?
(a) A'B' + C (b) (A' + B')C (c) A'B'C (d) (A + B)C'
**Answer: (b).** De Morgan: (AB)'·C = (A' + B')C.

**Q22.** AB + A'C + BC simplifies to:
(a) AB + BC (b) AB + A'C (c) A'C + BC (d) AB + A'C + BC
**Answer: (b).**

**Q23.** Dual of A(B + C)?
(a) A + BC (b) A'(B' + C') (c) AB + AC (d) A + B + C
**Answer: (a).**

**Q24.** XNOR output equals:
(a) AB' + A'B (b) AB + A'B' (c) A + B (d) (AB)'
**Answer: (b).** 1 when inputs match.

**Q25.** Cells in a 5-variable K-map?
(a) 16 (b) 25 (c) 32 (d) 64
**Answer: (c).** 2⁵.

**Q26.** Positive-logic NAND behaves as which gate in negative logic?
(a) NAND (b) NOR (c) AND (d) OR
**Answer: (b).** Swapping the meaning of 0 and 1 dualises the gate.

**Q27.** The majority function AB + BC + CA is:
(a) self-dual (b) its own complement (c) a universal gate (d) equivalent to XOR
**Answer: (a).** Its dual (A + B)(B + C)(C + A) expands back to AB + BC + CA.

**Q28.** In a 4-variable K-map, a group of 8 adjacent 1s gives a term with how many literals?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
**Answer: (a).** A group of 2ᵏ cells removes k variables: 4 − 3 = 1.

**Q29.** XOR of an odd number of 1-inputs gives:
(a) 0 (b) 1 (c) depends on order (d) undefined
**Answer: (b).** Multi-input XOR is the odd-parity function.

**Q30.** Minterms in a 3-variable function?
(a) 3 (b) 6 (c) 8 (d) 9
**Answer: (c).** 2³ input combinations.
