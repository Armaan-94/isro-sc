# 7.11 Transport Layer: Congestion Control, Timers and UDP

**Notes:** [11 Transport Layer Congestion Control and UDP](../../CN_Cybersecurity_ISRO_Notes/11_Transport_Layer_Congestion_Control_and_UDP.md)

> 24 questions. Slow start doubles cwnd each RTT up to ssthresh; then +1 MSS per RTT. Timeout: ssthresh = cwnd/2, cwnd = 1. Three duplicate ACKs: ssthresh = cwnd/2, cwnd = ssthresh.

---

**Q1.** cwnd starts at 1 MSS. After 4 RTTs of slow start?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 16 (d) 32 MSS
**Answer: (c).**

**Q2.** Timeout:
(a) ssthresh = cwnd/2, cwnd = ssthresh (b) ssthresh = cwnd/2, cwnd = 1 MSS, slow start again (c) double both (d) ignore
**Answer: (b).**

**Q3.** Prevents zero-window deadlock:
(a) keep-alive (b) time-wait (c) persistence timer (d) ACK timer
**Answer: (c).**

**Q4.** Slow start growth:
(a) linear (b) exponential (c) logarithmic (d) constant
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** Karn's algorithm ignores RTT samples of retransmitted segments because:
(a) corrupted (b) it's unclear which copy the ACK belongs to (c) no ACKs (d) saves time
**Answer: (b).** It doubles the timeout instead.

**Q6.** UDP header size:
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 20 (d) 16 bytes
**Answer: (b).**

**Q7.** Stops a sender from sending many tiny segments:
(a) Clark (b) Nagle (c) keep-alive (d) Karn
**Answer: (b).** Clark's solution fixes the receiver side.

**Q8.** UDP suits DNS and live media because:
(a) guaranteed delivery (b) low overhead, no retransmission delay (c) bigger header (d) TCP can't be used
**Answer: (b).**

**Q9.** Receiver window 8000 B, MSS 1000 B. ssthresh (by the given rule)?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 8 (d) 16
**Answer: (b).** (8000/1000)/2.

**Q10.** Sender window =
(a) min(receiver window, congestion window) (b) max of the two (c) receiver window only (d) cwnd only
**Answer: (a).**

**Q11.** cwnd = 1, ssthresh = 16 MSS. cwnd after 6 RTTs (no loss)?
(a) 16 (b) 17 (c) 18 (d) 32
**Answer: (c).** 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 after 4 RTTs; then 17, 18.

**Q12.** Same start; RTTs needed to reach cwnd = 32 MSS?
(a) 5 (b) 16 (c) 20 (d) 32
**Answer: (c).** 4 RTTs to 16, then 16 more at +1 per RTT.

**Q13.** Timeout when cwnd = 20 MSS. New ssthresh and cwnd?
(a) 10 and 1 (b) 10 and 10 (c) 20 and 1 (d) 5 and 1
**Answer: (a).**

**Q14.** Three duplicate ACKs when cwnd = 20 MSS. New ssthresh and cwnd?
(a) 10 and 1 (b) 10 and 10 (c) 20 and 10 (d) 5 and 5
**Answer: (b).** Fast recovery: resume linear growth.

**Q15.** TCP congestion control follows:
(a) AIMD (additive increase, multiplicative decrease) (b) MIAD (c) constant rate (d) random
**Answer: (a).**

**Q16.** Basic RTT estimate with α = 0.9, previous estimate 10 ms, measured 20 ms. New estimate and timeout (= 2 × estimate)?
(a) 11 ms, 22 ms (b) 19 ms, 38 ms (c) 15 ms, 30 ms (d) 10 ms, 20 ms
**Answer: (a).** 0.9 × 10 + 0.1 × 20.

**Q17.** Jacobson's algorithm improves timeouts by:
(a) adding the RTT deviation (timeout = RTT + 4 × deviation) (b) fixed 2× (c) ignoring RTT (d) using hop count
**Answer: (a).**

**Q18.** TIME-WAIT lasts:
(a) 2 × maximum segment lifetime (b) 1 RTT (c) forever (d) 0
**Answer: (a).**

**Q19.** The congestion window is:
(a) maintained by the sender and never sent on the wire (b) advertised in the TCP header (c) set by routers (d) the receiver's buffer
**Answer: (a).**

**Q20.** Applications that use UDP:
(a) DNS, DHCP, SNMP, TFTP, RIP (b) HTTP, FTP, SMTP (c) SSH, Telnet (d) BGP
**Answer: (a).**

**Q21.** UDP provides:
(a) no ordering, no reliability, no flow or congestion control (b) reliable ordered delivery (c) congestion control only (d) connections
**Answer: (a).** Only optional checksum and ports.

**Q22.** Maximum UDP payload (by the 16-bit length field, ignoring IP):
(a) 65,535 B (b) 65,527 B (c) 65,507 B (d) 1500 B
**Answer: (b).** 65,535 − 8. (Inside IPv4, 65,507 after the 20-byte IP header.)

**Q23.** Silly window syndrome causes:
(a) tiny segments with huge header overhead (b) congestion collapse always (c) deadlock (d) out-of-order delivery
**Answer: (a).**

**Q24.** Keep-alive timer:
(a) probes and closes idle dead connections (b) controls congestion (c) measures RTT (d) delays ACKs
**Answer: (a).**
