# 8.6 Software Quality, Maintenance and Risk Management

> 22 questions. Risk exposure = probability × loss. Availability = MTTF/(MTTF + MTTR). MTBF = MTTF + MTTR.

---

**Q1.** Largest share of maintenance effort:
(a) corrective (b) adaptive (c) perfective (d) preventive
**Answer: (c).**

**Q2.** Adaptive maintenance responds to:
(a) a bug (b) a changed environment (OS, hardware, regulation) (c) readability (d) proactive restructuring
**Answer: (b).**

**Q3.** CMM level where processes are measured quantitatively:
(a) 2 (b) 3 (c) 4 (d) 5
**Answer: (c).** Managed.

**Q4.** CMM levels in order:
(a) initial, defined, repeatable, managed, optimising (b) initial, repeatable, defined, managed, optimising (c) repeatable first (d) reverse
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** One of the six ISO 9126 characteristics:
(a) profitability (b) maintainability (c) marketability (d) scalability
**Answer: (b).** Also functionality, reliability, usability, efficiency, portability.

**Q6.** 30% probability, loss ₹2,00,000. Risk exposure?
(a) ₹2,00,000 (b) ₹60,000 (c) ₹30,000 (d) ₹2,30,000
**Answer: (b).**

**Q7.** Preventive maintenance:
(a) fixes a reported bug (b) proactively addresses latent issues (c) reacts to OS change (d) adds a feature
**Answer: (b).**

**Q8.** Risk management order:
(a) mitigation first (b) identification, analysis, prioritisation, planning, monitoring (c) monitoring first (d) analysis first
**Answer: (b).**

**Q9.** Business risk concerns:
(a) technical feasibility (b) market/organisational viability (c) schedule (d) compilers
**Answer: (b).**

**Q10.** Corrective maintenance is triggered by:
(a) regulation (b) a defect found after deployment (c) performance wishes (d) refactoring
**Answer: (b).**

**Q11.** MTTF = 90 hours, MTTR = 10 hours. Availability?
(a) 80% (b) 90% (c) 95% (d) 100%
**Answer: (b).** 90/100.

**Q12.** Same system. MTBF?
(a) 80 h (b) 90 h (c) 100 h (d) 9 h
**Answer: (c).**

**Q13.** Risk exposure ₹60,000 is reduced to ₹20,000 by a measure costing ₹10,000. Risk reduction leverage?
(a) 2 (b) 4 (c) 6 (d) 0.25
**Answer: (b).** (60,000 − 20,000)/10,000.

**Q14.** CMM level 2 (repeatable) introduces:
(a) basic project management so past successes can be repeated (b) quantitative control (c) continuous improvement (d) nothing
**Answer: (a).**

**Q15.** CMM level 5 is characterised by:
(a) continuous process improvement (b) chaos (c) only documentation (d) basic tracking
**Answer: (a).**

**Q16.** Six Sigma quality means about:
(a) 3.4 defects per million opportunities (b) 6 defects per hundred (c) zero defects (d) 1 defect per thousand
**Answer: (a).**

**Q17.** Reverse engineering is:
(a) recovering design/specification from existing code (b) writing code from scratch (c) testing (d) deployment
**Answer: (a).** Re-engineering then restructures and rebuilds.

**Q18.** Software configuration management controls:
(a) changes and versions of work products (b) hardware purchase (c) salaries (d) marketing
**Answer: (a).**

**Q19.** RMMM stands for:
(a) risk mitigation, monitoring and management (b) requirements, modelling, metrics, maintenance (c) reliability measurement model (d) none
**Answer: (a).**

**Q20.** Software reliability is:
(a) the probability of failure-free operation for a given time in a given environment (b) code size (c) the number of features (d) speed
**Answer: (a).**

**Q21.** Lehman's laws state that a widely used program:
(a) must keep changing or become progressively less useful, and its complexity grows unless worked against (b) never changes (c) shrinks over time (d) has constant complexity
**Answer: (a).**

**Q22.** ISO 9001 is a standard for:
(a) quality management systems (b) network protocols (c) programming languages (d) encryption
**Answer: (a).**
