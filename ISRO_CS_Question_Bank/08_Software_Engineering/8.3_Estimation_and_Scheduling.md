# 8.3 Project Estimation and Scheduling

> 22 questions. Basic COCOMO effort E = a(KLOC)ᵇ: organic 2.4, 1.05; semi-detached 3.0, 1.12; embedded 3.6, 1.20. PERT Tₑ = (O + 4M + P)/6, σ = (P − O)/6.

---

**Q1.** Organic project, 10 KLOC. Effort?
(a) 2.4 (b) 24 (c) about 26.9 (d) 240 person-months
**Answer: (c).** 2.4 × 10^1.05.

**Q2.** From organic to embedded, the exponent:
(a) stays the same (b) increases (c) decreases (d) irrelevant
**Answer: (b).**

**Q3.** Intermediate COCOMO adds:
(a) nothing about size (b) an effort adjustment factor from 15 cost drivers (c) removes modes (d) embedded only
**Answer: (b).**

**Q4.** PERT suits:
(a) repetitive, known projects (b) novel projects with uncertain durations (c) zero risk (d) one-day tasks
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** O = 4, M = 6, P = 14. PERT expected time?
(a) 6 (b) 7 (c) 8 (d) 24
**Answer: (b).**

**Q6.** The critical path is the:
(a) shortest path (b) longest path, fixing the minimum completion time (c) zero-cost path (d) fewest activities
**Answer: (b).**

**Q7.** Slack (float) is:
(a) project duration (b) how long an activity can slip without delaying the project (c) resources (d) probability
**Answer: (b).**

**Q8.** Small in-house team, familiar requirements:
(a) embedded (b) semi-detached (c) organic (d) detailed
**Answer: (c).**

**Q9.** Critical path A–B–C (5, 8, 6 days); a parallel path totals 15 days. Completion time?
(a) 15 (b) 19 (c) 6 (d) 29
**Answer: (b).**

**Q10.** Detailed COCOMO adds:
(a) ignores drivers (b) phase-by-phase estimation (c) organic only (d) no KLOC
**Answer: (b).**

**Q11.** Same activity as Q5. Standard deviation?
(a) 1 (b) about 1.67 (c) 10 (d) 2.78
**Answer: (b).** (14 − 4)/6. Variance ≈ 2.78.

**Q12.** O = 2, M = 5, P = 14. Expected time?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 7 (d) 21
**Answer: (b).** (2 + 20 + 14)/6.

**Q13.** Network: A (3) precedes B (4) and C (6); both precede D (2). Project duration?
(a) 9 (b) 11 (c) 15 (d) 13
**Answer: (b).** Paths A–B–D = 9, A–C–D = 11.

**Q14.** In Q13, slack of B?
(a) 0 (b) 2 (c) 4 (d) 6
**Answer: (b).** 11 − 9.

**Q15.** In Q13, critical activities?
(a) A, B, D (b) A, C, D (c) B, C (d) all
**Answer: (b).**

**Q16.** 20 KLOC at a productivity of 500 LOC per person-month. Effort?
(a) 10 PM (b) 40 PM (c) 100 PM (d) 4 PM
**Answer: (b).**

**Q17.** Same project with 5 developers. Duration (ignoring overheads)?
(a) 4 months (b) 8 months (c) 10 months (d) 20 months
**Answer: (b).**

**Q18.** Brooks's law:
(a) adding people to a late project makes it later (b) more people always speeds things up (c) testing finds all bugs (d) software grows linearly
**Answer: (a).**

**Q19.** A Gantt chart shows:
(a) tasks as bars along a time axis (b) dependencies only (c) costs (d) risks
**Answer: (a).** A PERT/CPM network emphasises dependencies.

**Q20.** Basic COCOMO development time formula for organic mode:
(a) Tdev = 2.5 E^0.38 (b) Tdev = 2.4 E^1.05 (c) Tdev = E/2.5 (d) Tdev = 3.6 E^1.2
**Answer: (a).** Exponents 0.35 (semi-detached), 0.32 (embedded).

**Q21.** Basic COCOMO estimates effort from:
(a) estimated KLOC (b) function points only (c) team size only (d) number of tests
**Answer: (a).**

**Q22.** CPM time estimates are:
(a) deterministic (single value) (b) probabilistic (three values) (c) random (d) not used
**Answer: (a).**
