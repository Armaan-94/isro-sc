# 2.13 Graphs: Representation, DFS, BFS and Topological Sort

> 28 questions. Neighbours are visited in increasing order unless stated.

---

**Q1.** BFS uses a ___; DFS uses a ___:
(a) stack, stack (b) queue, stack/recursion (c) stack, queue (d) priority queue for both
**Answer: (b).**

**Q2.** Shortest path (fewest edges) in an unweighted graph:
(a) DFS (b) BFS (c) both (d) neither
**Answer: (b).**

**Q3.** DFS on a directed graph finds a back edge. Conclusion?
(a) it's a tree (b) bipartite (c) it has a cycle (d) disconnected
**Answer: (c).**

**Q4.** Topological sort is possible only on:
(a) any directed graph (b) a DAG (c) undirected graphs (d) trees only
**Answer: (b).**

**Q5.** In Kahn's algorithm a vertex is output when its:
(a) out-degree is 0 (b) in-degree is 0 (c) number is smallest (d) neighbours are visited
**Answer: (b).** If vertices remain but none has in-degree 0, there's a cycle.

**Q6.** For a sparse graph, the space-efficient representation is:
(a) adjacency matrix (b) adjacency list (c) equal (d) incidence matrix
**Answer: (b).** O(V + E) vs O(V²).

**Q7.** Strongly connected components are meaningful for:
(a) undirected graphs (b) directed graphs (c) both equally (d) trees
**Answer: (b).** Found by Kosaraju (two DFS passes) or Tarjan (one pass).

**Q8.** DFS edge that connects unrelated subtrees:
(a) back (b) tree (c) cross (d) forward
**Answer: (c).**

**Q9.** BFS and DFS with an adjacency list run in:
(a) O(V²) (b) O(V + E) (c) O(E log V) (d) O(V log V)
**Answer: (b).** O(V²) with an adjacency matrix.

**Q10.** Maximum edges in a simple undirected graph with n vertices?
(a) n − 1 (b) n(n − 1)/2 (c) n² (d) n(n − 1)
**Answer: (b).** n(n − 1) for directed.

**Q11.** Degrees 3, 3, 2, 2, 2. Number of edges?
(a) 5 (b) 6 (c) 12 (d) 10
**Answer: (b).** Sum of degrees = 2E = 12.

**Q12.** In any graph, the number of odd-degree vertices is:
(a) always odd (b) always even (c) zero (d) any number
**Answer: (b).** The degree sum is even.

**Q13.** A tree with n vertices has:
(a) n edges (b) n − 1 edges (c) n + 1 edges (d) n/2 edges
**Answer: (b).**

**Q14.** Number of spanning trees of K₄?
(a) 4 (b) 8 (c) 12 (d) 16
**Answer: (d).** Cayley's formula nⁿ⁻².

**Q15.** Edges 1-2, 1-3, 2-4, 2-5, 3-6, 3-7, start at 1. DFS order?
(a) 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 (b) 1 2 4 5 3 6 7 (c) 1 3 7 6 2 5 4 (d) 1 2 4 3 5 6 7
**Answer: (b).**

**Q16.** Same graph. BFS order?
(a) 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 (b) 1 2 4 5 3 6 7 (c) 1 3 2 7 6 5 4 (d) 1 2 4 3 6 5 7
**Answer: (a).**

**Q17.** DAG with edges a→b, a→c, b→d, c→d. Number of topological orders?
(a) 1 (b) 2 (c) 3 (d) 4
**Answer: (b).** a, b, c, d and a, c, b, d.

**Q18.** A graph is bipartite if and only if it has no:
(a) even cycle (b) odd cycle (c) cycle (d) vertex of degree 1
**Answer: (b).** Test with BFS 2-colouring.

**Q19.** Edges in K₃,₄ (complete bipartite)?
(a) 7 (b) 12 (c) 21 (d) 6
**Answer: (b).** m × n.

**Q20.** Every DAG has at least one vertex with:
(a) in-degree 0 (b) out-degree 2 (c) a self-loop (d) degree n − 1
**Answer: (a).** Also at least one with out-degree 0.

**Q21.** A connected graph has an Euler circuit if and only if:
(a) all vertices have even degree (b) exactly two odd-degree vertices (c) it's complete (d) it's a tree
**Answer: (a).** Exactly two odd vertices gives an Euler path but not a circuit.

**Q22.** Deciding whether a Hamiltonian cycle exists is:
(a) O(V + E) (b) NP-complete (c) O(V²) (d) undecidable
**Answer: (b).**

**Q23.** A vertex whose removal disconnects a connected graph is:
(a) a bridge (b) an articulation point (cut vertex) (c) a leaf (d) a sink
**Answer: (b).** A bridge is an edge with that property.

**Q24.** In DFS of an undirected graph, the possible edge types are:
(a) tree and back only (b) tree, back, forward, cross (c) cross only (d) tree only
**Answer: (a).**

**Q25.** Topological order from DFS is:
(a) increasing finish times (b) decreasing finish times (c) discovery order (d) BFS order
**Answer: (b).**

**Q26.** A DFS tree of a connected graph with V vertices has:
(a) V edges (b) V − 1 edges (c) E edges (d) E − V edges
**Answer: (b).**

**Q27.** The adjacency matrix of an undirected graph is always:
(a) symmetric (b) upper triangular (c) diagonal (d) identity
**Answer: (a).**

**Q28.** Counting connected components of an undirected graph:
(a) needs Dijkstra (b) run BFS/DFS from every unvisited vertex; count the starts (c) impossible in linear time (d) needs MST
**Answer: (b).** O(V + E).
